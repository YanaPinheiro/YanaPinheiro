### 🔬 About My Research

I am currently part of the graduate program at **CPGF-UFPA** and work as a researcher at **INCT da Criosfera** (Glacier Geophysics). My main academic and technical research areas include:
- 🧊 **Cryospheric Geophysics:** Processing of microseismic monitoring data, GPR, and modeling of wind-induced seismic signals in polar environments (Antarctica).
- 🧠 **Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning:** Application of Generative Adversarial Networks (**GANs**) for noise attenuation in seismic data and automated rock/facies classification.
- 📊 **Signal Processing:** Development of computational routines and pipelines for geophysical data interpretation.
